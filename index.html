Dear Abhishek,

Happy Birthday mere bhai ❤️

Yaar, dosti to bahut logon se hoti hai,
lekin tere jaisa dost kismat walon ko milta hai.
Tu mere liye sirf dost nahi, bhai hai.

College ke woh funny moments,
woh shararatein, woh hasi-mazaak,
aur har musibat me saath khade rehna,
ye sab yaadein hamesha dil me rahengi.

Jab bhi zindagi ne mushkil raste dikhaye,
tu har baar mere saath khada mila.
Isi liye teri dosti meri zindagi ki sabse keemti cheezon me se ek hai.

Main dua karta hu ki teri har khwahish puri ho,
teri zindagi khushiyon se bhari rahe,
aur tu hamesha isi tarah hasta-muskurata rahe.

Waqt badal sakta hai, log badal sakte hain,
lekin meri dua hai ki hamari dosti kabhi na badle. ♾️❤️
